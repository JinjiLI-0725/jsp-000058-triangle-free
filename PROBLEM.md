# JSP-000058

Let G be a triangle-free graph on 5k vertices.

Define

d(G) = |E(G)| - MaxCut(G).

Equivalently, d(G) is the minimum number of edges that must be deleted from G to make G bipartite.

The conjecture asks whether every triangle-free graph on 5k vertices satisfies

d(G) <= k^2.

Computational evidence is not a proof.
